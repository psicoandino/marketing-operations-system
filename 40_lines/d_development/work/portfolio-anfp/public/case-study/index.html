Marketing Data Pipeline & Dashboard — Case Study

What this case is

An operator was responsible for a substantial part of the marketing data pipeline at a real organization, Universidad de Chile, Educación Ejecutiva — consolidating information from more than five heterogeneous operational sources into a single dependable contact base at a scale above 250,000 active contacts and 400,000 historical records. This case study reconstructs that work for public demonstration; it is not an export of the original system.

The problem

Contact and enrollment data lived scattered across heterogeneous, disconnected operational sources, requiring repeated manual work before any of it was usable for segmentation, campaigns, or reporting.

The reconstruction

The reconstruction is split into two linked, self-contained demonstrations, each addressing one stage of the same story and each disclosing its own synthetic data: a Pipeline View, covering data consolidation, and a Dashboard View, covering operational visibility into the prospect lifecycle.

What to look at first

Open the Pipeline View to see heterogeneous sources cleaned, normalized, and consolidated into one contact base. Open the Dashboard View to see how that consolidated base supports visibility into where prospects stand, from acquisition through enrollment.

What this is not

This is not the original organizational system, and no production data is exported or displayed. Both linked demonstrations run entirely on synthetic data. Nothing here claims a specific measured business outcome, real-time reporting, or permanent production use of any experimental model.
