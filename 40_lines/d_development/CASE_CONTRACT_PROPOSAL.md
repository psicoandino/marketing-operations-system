Status

PROPOSED

Derived from

Promotion condition

This proposal may become a contract only after repeated use provides evidence
that its claims are necessary, sufficient and non-contradictory.

The operator decides promotion after an independent audit.

## Purpose

A Case exists to demonstrate a capability through evidence.

A Case organizes evidence about a demonstrated capability and may produce an
exportable professional artifact.

---

## A Case is NOT

- a curriculum entry;
- a project diary;
- documentation;
- a blog article;
- a portfolio page.

Those may be produced from a Case.

They are not the Case itself.

---

## Every Case contains

One canonical state.

One evidence directory.

One public artifact (optional).

One closure condition.

---

## Authority

Recovered evidence has precedence.

Operator-confirmed testimony must remain explicitly identified.

Model inference never promotes a claim.

---

## Evolution

Evidence may create derived documents.

Derived documents never replace canonical evidence.

The canonical state always points to the evidence from which it derives.

---

## Completion

A Case is complete when a public artifact can be exported without
contradicting its evidence.

Completion is judged by the closure condition defined inside the Case.

---

## Review

Changes to this contract require demonstrated contradiction from an existing
Case.

Elegance is not sufficient.
