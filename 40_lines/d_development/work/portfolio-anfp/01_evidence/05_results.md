# Case 01 — Results

## Authority

Operator-verified testimony.

No original performance reports, time studies, conversion analyses or financial
impact records have yet been recovered.

## Operational changes

The work changed the operating process from fragmented and repeatedly manual
data preparation toward a recurring consolidated flow.

Before the pipeline:

- data arrived through disconnected systems and files;
- records required repeated manual preparation;
- duplicate and inconsistent data reduced usability;
- segmentation and reporting depended on fragmented information;
- campaign metrics required separate manual treatment.

After implementation:

- contact information was consolidated into a central working base;
- recurring cleaning, normalization and deduplication became part of the flow;
- campaign audiences could be constructed from prepared data;
- campaign metrics could be transformed into internally usable structures;
- dashboard source data could be refreshed hourly.

## Capabilities created

The work created or materially strengthened the ability to:

- combine information from more than five operational sources;
- maintain a prepared contact base at a scale above 250,000 active contacts;
- preserve and analyze more than 400,000 historical records;
- construct operational segments using business rules;
- use association rules operationally;
- test a Random Forest model in operational conditions;
- process campaign metrics exported from GroupMail Insights;
- update Google Sheets and Looker Studio through an hourly automated flow.

## Observable outcomes

Operator-observed outcomes included:

- more centralized marketing and communications information;
- less repetitive manual preparation;
- greater continuity in campaign data processing;
- improved ability to construct campaign audiences;
- campaign metrics available for internal analysis;
- recurring operational visibility through Looker Studio;
- hourly refresh of dashboard source data.

These outcomes are operationally credible but are not yet supported by recovered
independent measurements.

## Claims supported

The portfolio may state that the operator:

- designed and operated a multi-source marketing data pipeline;
- consolidated and prepared data from heterogeneous sources;
- automated cleaning, normalization and deduplication;
- supported segmentation through business rules, association rules and an
  experimentally operational Random Forest model;
- processed campaign metrics for internal reporting;
- automated hourly data updates to Google Sheets and Looker Studio;
- worked at a scale above 250,000 active contacts and 400,000 historical
  records.

## Claims prohibited

The portfolio must not claim:

- a specific percentage reduction in manual work;
- a specific duplicate-reduction rate;
- a specific increase in campaign conversion;
- a specific increase in revenue;
- a specific model accuracy;
- guaranteed contact quality;
- real-time reporting;
- fully automated campaign execution;
- permanent production use of the Random Forest model;
- current production availability of the system.

## Evidence limits

The following evidence has not yet been recovered:

- before-and-after time measurements;
- duplicate-rate measurements;
- conversion or campaign uplift analyses;
- revenue attribution;
- model evaluation reports;
- campaign performance reports;
- dashboard screenshots or exports;
- system logs;
- manager or colleague confirmation.

Therefore, all results remain classified as operator-verified operational
outcomes rather than independently measured impact.
