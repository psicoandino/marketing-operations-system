# 01_DERIVATION — Complete derivation chains

Every element of `001_psicoandino-os/` exists because surviving evidence
requires it. This document shows, per decision: the evidence chain, why it
cannot be removed, why no generic substitute works, and what its loss would
cost. Elements that could not be derived were **not built** — they are listed
at the end, because their absence is also derived.

Convention: [O] operator-spoken/raw · [F] operator-frozen document ·
[B] behaviorally confirmed across strata · [P] derivation-pipeline finding
(2026-07-19/20; model testimony, cited as witness).

---

## D0 — Existence, name, and number

**Chain:** The operator's own numbering has a vacant slot: `000_psicoandino`
(site) and `002_psico-header-v1.0-frozen` exist; `001_` does not [F]. The
name "Sistema Operativo Psicoandino" is the operator's own title for his
context layer (CONTEXT.md header; concilio skill description) [F]. This
environment fills the operator's own gap with the operator's own term.
**Cannot be removed:** the corpus has no operating habitat — it has a
philosophy (archive), a method (external strata), a site (000), and no place
where they operate together; the AUDITORIA of 2026-07-03 and the
Reconstruction both documented that absence as lived disorientation
("lost, lost, lost" [O]).
**Generic test:** a generic lab starts a repo; it does not inherit a
numbering gap, a Spanish OS title, or five open refutations at birth.

## D1 — `00_seed/` as exempt core

**Chain:** Seed derivation → Seed destruction [P] reduced the foundation to
identity particulars whose loss is permanent (operator-spoken formulations
[O], operator-raw palette [O], provenance kernel). The corpus's own law "no
parafrasear" [F] and the observed selection rule — only unrefutable elements
persisted across all strata [B] — make these the identity carriers.
**Cannot be removed:** everything else in the environment is regenerable;
section A is not. Removing it removes the only thing that makes a future
rebuild *Psicoandino* rather than a similar practice.
**No generic substitute:** no generic organization has "capatencial" or
"certeza ambigua desconfiada central"; a mission statement is replaceable,
these words are not.
**Embedded provenance (A9):** the Seed destruction proved that without the
source document inside, every [O] status becomes self-assertion. Hence
`provenance/extraccion_identidad_v0.2.md` is copied in (source stratum
untouched — custody, not migration).

## D2 — The dual ledger (`30_ledger/` VALUE_EVENTS ≠ DEPOSITS)

**Chain:** Value definition, exempt [O]: *funcionamiento orgánico generador
de bienestar* — wellbeing occurs in someone. Canon completion test asks
"Whose wellbeing changed?" [F]. Behavioral record: every completed closure in
the corpus was judged by the author or a machine; zero external closures
exist, stated by the corpus itself ("nadie externo ha pagado, leído ni usado
nada aún") [F] and verified independently [P] (no git remote, placeholder
content, unset price fields). Epistemology audit [P]: nine of ten possible
objectives can be satisfied by self-judged artifacts; only value cannot.
**Cannot be removed:** without the split, the environment cannot distinguish
its success from its documented failure mode; every previous system of the
corpus lacked this distinction and each one drowned in its own deposits.
**No generic substitute:** generic labs count output. This ledger counts
*judges*. It is the corpus's own axiology made structural.
**Loss if it disappeared:** the refuge pattern becomes invisible again —
the exact condition under which four systems were serially founded and
abandoned in one month.

## D3 — The two closure classes and the otherness gate (LAW §3)

**Chain:** Class M precedent: Radiomisor, "verificado con mis oídos", hashes,

recognizes.
**Not generic:** no generic methodology needs a law that *forbids counting
self-verified work as value* — this law is derivable only from this corpus's
specific, documented boundary.

## FINAL TEST

**"Could this operating environment have been derived without the Psicoandino
corpus?"**

Remove the corpus and the derivation fails at every load-bearing point:

- `00_seed/` becomes empty — no other source contains these words, this
  palette, this provenance.
- The dual ledger loses its reason — without the exempt value definition and
  the documented self-judgment boundary, DEPOSITS/VALUE_EVENTS collapses into
  a generic changelog.
- LAW §3's two classes lose their precedents (ears-verified radio; the three
  halted fields) — underivable from any generic lab.
- VOICES.md loses both its six questions and the recorded failure that
  justifies the balance rule.
- The five birth-refutations vanish — a generic environment starts "clean,"
  which is exactly the false start this corpus made four times.
- Substitute "Bell Labs" or "OpenAI" for "Psicoandino" and D0, D1, D2, D3,
  D4, D7 fail immediately: their organizations have budgets, teams, and
  external judges by default — the central laws of this environment would be
  meaningless to them.

What remains without the corpus is: folders, append-only files, and role
separation — the generic ~20% (route's economy, envelope hygiene, archive
append-only), which the corpus itself imported from engineering culture and
which carries none of the identity.

The environment is derivable from this corpus and only from this corpus.
