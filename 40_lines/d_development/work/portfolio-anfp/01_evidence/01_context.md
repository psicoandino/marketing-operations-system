# Case 01 — Context

## Authority

Operator-verified testimony.

No original organizational documentation has yet been recovered.

## Organization

Universidad de Chile

Educación Ejecutiva

Departamento de Control de Gestión y Sistemas de Información

## Operator role

Marketing and Communication Data Analyst.

The operator was responsible for a substantial part of the marketing data
pipeline used to prepare, consolidate and analyze marketing and communications information.

## Operational context

Marketing and communications information arrived from multiple operational systems, campaign
providers, forms and manually managed files.

The information was heterogeneous, duplicated and difficult to consolidate
into a reliable marketing database.

The organization required recurring campaign execution, segmentation and
operational reporting from those sources.

## Scale

Operator-confirmed:

- more than 250,000 active contacts;
- more than 400,000 historical records;
- more than five operational data sources;
- recurring daily operation.

## Problem statement

The organization lacked a unified, consistently prepared marketing data
foundation suitable for segmentation, campaign execution and reporting.

Multiple disconnected sources required repeated manual work before they could
be used operationally.

## Why this work mattered

Without a consolidated and prepared marketing dataset:

- segmentation quality deteriorated;
- campaign preparation became repetitive;
- reporting required additional manual processing;
- operational visibility was reduced.

## Evidence limits

This document is based on operator-confirmed testimony.

Original repositories, database schemas, screenshots and internal
documentation have not yet been recovered.

- the contact base exceeded 250,000 active contacts;
